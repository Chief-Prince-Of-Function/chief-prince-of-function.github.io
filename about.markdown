## Intro
I’m Michael Fusco - husband, father, veteran, founder, and builder. I’m grateful for where I’ve been, grounded in where I am, and committed to building what comes next with purpose.

## My Story
My life wasn’t built in a straight line. It’s been shaped by responsibility, service, hard lessons, and the kind of moments that force you to decide who you’re going to be.

I’m a husband and a father first. Everything else builds on that. Those roles demand consistency, not talk. Those roles taught me that showing up, day after day, matters more than any single win.

My time in service and the years after gave me a clear lens: most people don’t need more ideas, they need better execution. That’s where I’ve found my place. I build systems, tools, and workflows that bring order to chaos. I build things that actually get used, not just talked about.

I’ve made mistakes. I’ve had to reset. I’ve had to rebuild parts of my life with more humility and discipline than I started with. That process never really ends and I don’t expect it to.

What drives me now is simple:
be useful, stay grounded, and keep improving.

Whether it’s my work, my family, or the things I build, I care about doing things the right way, for the right reasons, and leaving things better than I found them.

## What I’m Building
I’m currently focused on work that combines practical value with long term purpose:

- **RiverLog** — a fishing log built to help anglers track their time on the water and improve over time.
- **Veterans Go Outdoors** — a nonprofit centered on connection, healing, and community through meaningful outdoor experiences.
- **Digital builder work** — dashboards, tools, and small products that sharpen my full stack development skills while solving real problems.

## Why I Build
I don’t build for hype. I build because useful tools can make life better, reduce friction, and serve people well. Craftsmanship matters to me. Doing honest work, refining details, and staying teachable through every phase of the process is critical to success and growth.

## Values
Faith. Family. Discipline. Gratitude. Service. These are not slogans for me, they are the standards I try to live by.
