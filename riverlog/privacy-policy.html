Privacy Policy for RiverLog

RiverLog does not collect, store, or share personal data.

All information entered into the app, including trips, catches, notes, and photos, is stored locally on your device. RiverLog does not transmit your data to external servers.

Optional features such as GPS location and photos are used only to enhance your personal logging experience and remain on your device unless you choose to export them.

RiverLog does not use third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.
