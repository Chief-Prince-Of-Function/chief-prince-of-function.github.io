I founded Veterans Go Outdoors in 2018 after a difficult period in my life. In a military rehabilitation program, I sat with people from every branch of service and heard them talk about what they were carrying. I remember how much it meant to be able to speak honestly with people who understood. The way those veterans showed up for one another made me want to do the same for someone else.

Fly fishing gave me a way to do that. I remember getting ready to take a group of veterans fly fishing in the Blue Ridge Mountains of North Carolina. I hoped to catch fish, of course. What I was really looking forward to was standing in the river together and spending time with people who understood what that company could mean.

Today, Veterans Go Outdoors brings veterans together through retreats, fly fishing, golf, and shared time outside. It’s the work I’ve been doing the longest, and one of the greatest blessings in my life. I’m grateful for the people who helped me get here and for every chance to spend a day outside with other veterans.
